## State and secrets

Terraform state for both stages lives in `fau-tfstate`, versioned, with permanent deletion denied
by a bucket policy (`s3:DeleteObjectVersion`, `s3:DeleteBucket`, `s3:PutBucketVersioning`). It
holds the admin SSH key, six WireGuard private keys and the SOPS age key in clear text. There is
no copy outside Hetzner, decided deliberately: the keys are regenerable and account loss would
take the infrastructure too. The one revisit trigger is GitOps secrets being encrypted to the age
key. Exporting the keys to Proton Pass closes that, tracked on #3481.

Credentials and generated files live only in the `infra-runtime` volume at 0700/0600:
`.credentials.tfvars`, `.backend.hcl`, `favro.env`, and under `.config/` the SSH keypair,
`persistent_outputs.json`, `bootstrap_outputs.json`, `kubeconfig.yaml`, `node-token` and
`fau.conf`. Never use `docker compose down -v`.

## Decisions that constrain the next steps

- **Nodes**: three identical CX23. The CX category flickers in and out of stock, which is why
  stage 1 was applied when it was. CX33 (8 GB, EUR 8.49) is priced but out of stock in every EU
  datacenter; upgrading later is an in-place rescale plus reboot, x86 to x86, disk 40 to 80 GB.
- **Volume cost**: the EUR 14.30–15.35 estimate is accepted; `loki` and `tempo` stay unpinned and
  `helm` is not being added for costing. The first invoice is the authority.
- **Email**: managed European transactional service, self-hosted SMTP ruled out, Scaleway TEM
  Essential provisional for dev/test/pilot; production pending the verification list on #3410.
- **Suppliers**: customer-facing must be European; internal may not be. Slack, ntfy and SMTP2GO
  are upstream examples, not adopted, and recommended for dropping.
- **VPN**: WireGuard, chosen on effort and cost. Tailscale assessed and parked in archived #3440.
- **Language**: all technical content in English. The product is Bokmål with Nynorsk support as
  an architecture requirement; never author Nynorsk content.
